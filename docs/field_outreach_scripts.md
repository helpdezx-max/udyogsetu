## 2. Integrity & Truth-in-Advertising Rules
- **NEVER** claim the platform already has active placements or agreements with an employer until the agreement or registration is completed.
- **NEVER** claim that every company in Chakan is on the platform. State clearly: *"UdyogSetu is an expanding local employment network in Pune District."*
- **NEVER** guarantee that 100% of posted vacancies will be filled. Promise verified exposure, zero candidate fee protection, and automated interview scheduling.
