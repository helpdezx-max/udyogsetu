## 3. Simplified, Zero-Friction Candidate Onboarding

To maximize adoption among factory technicians, onboarding removes all unnecessary barriers:

```
[1. Mobile / WhatsApp Entry] ➔ [2. Basic Verification] ➔ [3. Structured Profile] ➔ [4. Instant Job Discovery] ➔ [5. One-Click Apply]
```

### Critical UX Safeguards:
1. **NO MANDATORY RESUME UPLOAD:**
   - Frontline technicians rarely possess up-to-date PDF resumes on mobile devices.
   - A structured 4-step profile (Trade, Machine Controllers, Experience, Preferred Cluster) is 100% sufficient for full application submission.
2. **100% FREE FOREVER FOR CANDIDATES:**
   - Registration, profile creation, application, and interview gate passes remain strictly zero-cost.
   - Any attempt by a recruiter to solicit fees triggers automatic platform quarantine and legal reporting.
3. **Vernacular Support:**
   - Seamless toggling between **Marathi (`mr`)**, **Hindi (`hi`)**, and **English (`en`)** throughout the candidate portal and WhatsApp notifications.
