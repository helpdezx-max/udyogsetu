**Hyperlocal Industrial Employment Network for Chakan MIDC & Pune Industrial Corridor**

## 1. Executive Introduction
**UdyogSetu (उद्योगसेतू)** is a specialized industrial recruitment and workforce verification platform built specifically for manufacturing units operating in **Chakan MIDC (Phases 1, 2, 3, 4), Khed City, Kuruli, Mahalunge, and the Pune industrial belt**.

Our mission is to eliminate ghost vacancies, middleman extortion, and high technician drop-out rates by directly connecting factory HR teams with pre-verified local technicians, ITI apprentices, and experienced machine operators.

## 3. Step-by-Step Employer Onboarding Guide

```
[1. Online Registration] ➔ [2. Statutory Verification] ➔ [3. Post Vacancy] ➔ [4. Review Applicants] ➔ [5. Gate Interview] ➔ [6. Placement]
```

### Step 1: Online Registration
- Navigate to `https://udyogsetu-e7o1.vercel.app/employer/register`
- Enter official company name, primary plant address, plant HR contact details, and create secure credentials.

### Step 2: Statutory Verification Audit
To safeguard candidates from fraudulent recruiters, all manufacturing units must upload:
1. **Udyam Registration Certificate** (Ministry of MSME)
2. **GSTIN Certificate** (State of Maharashtra - 27...)
3. **Factory License / MIDC Plot Allotment Letter**
*Verification is reviewed and approved by UdyogSetu compliance officers within 24 business hours.*

### Step 3: Vacancy Submission
Submit job specifications through our structured industrial vacancy desk:
- Specify Trade / Specialization (e.g. CNC Operator, VMC Programmer, Welder, Fitter)
- Outline Shift Schedule & Overtime policies (compliant with Maharashtra Factories Act)
- Detail PPE and Plant Amenities (Subsidized Canteen, Safety Shoes, Bus Pick-and-Drop)
- Disclose transparent gross and net in-hand monthly salary breakdown.

### Step 4: Candidate Screening & Shortlisting
- Plant HR receives direct notifications (WhatsApp & Web Dashboard) when matching candidates apply.
- Review candidate's trade certificates, machine controller experience (Fanuc, Siemens, Heidenhain), and current residence.

### Step 5: Gate Interview & Shopfloor Assessment
- Issue pre-authorized Gate Pass interview slots with one click.
- Candidates receive automated WhatsApp invitations with exact plant gate location and reporting instructions.

### Step 6: Offer & Verified Placement Confirmation
- Record interview outcomes and extend formal digital offers on the platform.
- Dual confirmation upon candidate joining ensures accurate reporting and dispute resolution.
